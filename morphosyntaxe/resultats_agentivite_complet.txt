there are several reasons a name gets "indéterminé":

Name appears alone without nearby pronouns (e.g. just "Schulmann" in a sentence with no "il" or "elle" nearby)
Ambiguous names that could be male or female (e.g. "Alex", "Claude", "Dominique")
Foreign names spaCy doesn't know the gender of
Abbreviations or nicknames

It's not a bug — it's genuinely uncertain from the text context alone.

CONCLUSION
=================================================================
  Humans act as agents 37% of the time.
  Animals act as agents 24% of the time — 1.5x less than humans.
  Plants act as agents only 11.8% — 3x less than humans.
  Plants appear predominantly as noun complements (49.5%),
  describing things rather than doing them.
  Male and female humans show nearly identical agentivity (38.2% vs 37.7%).

  → The hierarchy Humans > Animals > Plants is confirmed
    across 379,163 total mentions in 925 French novels.
